Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

NEXOR is a platform for corporate experiences in the City of London. For questions about personal information, please reach us through our contact page.

Information we collect

When you submit a corporate enquiry or partner application, we may collect your name, company details, work contact details and the information you choose to provide. Our website may also receive basic technical information needed to operate securely.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, assess partnership applications, arrange requested services and keep the website secure. Depending on the circumstances, our legal basis may be taking steps at your request before a contract, legitimate interests or a legal obligation.

Sharing and storage

We may use trusted hosting and form-processing providers to operate the website and handle enquiries. We do not sell your personal information. We retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

Under applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your information, restrict or object to its use, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Contact us to make a request.

Cookies and updates

We use a browser storage preference to remember light or dark mode. Other cookies or analytics, if introduced, should be described here before use. We may update this policy; any changes will appear on this page.
